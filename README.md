## ✨ What Is LabLLM?

LabLLM is a friendly, visual playground for your Mac that lets you build, train, and watch a tiny language model — the same technology behind ChatGPT — grow from a blank slate into something that can actually "think" and generate text. 

Think of it like a science lab for artificial intelligence. You get to see every gear turn, watch the numbers adjust, and understand exactly how a chatbot learns to speak. No coding experience needed. If you can click a button and drag a slider, you can use LabLLM.

This app is designed specifically for Apple Silicon Macs (M1, M2, M3, or newer). It uses your Mac's built-in brain power to run everything locally — no cloud, no internet, no data leaving your computer. Your experiments are private and fast.

## 💡 What Can You Do With LabLLM?

LabLLM isn't just a demo. It's a full laboratory. Here's what you can explore:

- **🧱 Build the Architecture** – Choose how many layers, neurons, and attention heads your model should have. See a visual diagram update in real time as you tweak the settings.
- **📚 Train on Your Own Data** – Feed the model any text — a book, your journal, song lyrics, or a custom dataset you create. The model learns from exactly what you give it.
- **🎛️ Tune the Learning Process** – Adjust learning rates, batch sizes, and training loops. Watch live graphs show loss curves and accuracy as your model improves.
- **🔧 Fine-Tune With LoRA** – Use advanced LoRA techniques to specialize your model on a particular style or task without retraining everything from scratch.
- **💾 Save and Resume Checkpoints** – Pause your training, save the exact state of your model, and come back later. Your progress is always safe.
- **🔍 Quantize for Speed** – Squeeze your model to run faster and use less memory with quantization — a key trick used by real-world AI engineers.
- **📝 Watch Text Generation Live** – After training, type a prompt and watch your tiny LLM respond. See the token-by-token predictions in real time.
- **📊 Understand the Math** – Visualize how attention works, see weight distributions, and inspect the internal representations the model builds.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I need to know Python or coding?
No. LabLLM is a native macOS app with a graphical interface. Everything is done through buttons, sliders, and text fields.

### Will this hurt my Mac or slow it down?
LabLLM uses the same MLX framework that powers professional ML tools on Apple Silicon. It's designed to be safe. Training large models may use significant memory, so monitor your Activity Monitor if you push the limits.

### Can I use my own text files?
Absolutely. Drag and drop `.txt`, `.csv`, or `.json` files into the data panel, or paste text directly.

### Is my data sent anywhere?
No. Everything runs on your device. No telemetry, no cloud, no sharing.

### How long does training take?
With a small dataset (a few paragraphs), training can complete in seconds. With larger datasets (a book), it might take a few minutes. The app shows progress bars and estimated time.
